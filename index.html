About Us

Hensational started in a Northern Virginia kitchen with one question: why does performance food have to taste like cardboard and read like a chemistry set. Built by a wrestler who trains hard and eats clean, every bar leans on whole food ingredients, real fruit, real cacao, real tallow, so it fuels a workout instead of just filling a wrapper. Nut free by design, so it belongs in every gym bag and every lunchbox.
